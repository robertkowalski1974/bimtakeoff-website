Frequently asked questions

Q: What do you need from me to start?

A: Drawings as PDF or DWG, the specification or NBS output, the tender bill if there is one, and your return date. We read IFC from Revit, ArchiCAD and Tekla if you have a model, but we do not need one — most of what we measure comes off 2D drawings.

Q: Is my package too small?

A: No. Most of what we do is single trade packages.

Q: What format does the takeoff or priced bill come back in?

A: Yours. Excel in your estimating layout, a CITE/eBQ file for import, or the bill format your main contractor has issued. Tell us what your estimator works in and we deliver to it.

Q: What does it cost?

A: Priced per package, quoted before we start. No hourly billing and no fee for scope we did not agree.

Q: How accurate are the quantities?

A: Quantities are measured to the drawings and the specification you send. Where the information is incomplete we list the assumption rather than absorb it.
