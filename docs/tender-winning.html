Where subcontract tenders come unstuck

Before anyone looks at your price

A return can be set aside before the price is opened at all. The usual causes are the dull ones: a missing form, a qualification that reads as a departure from the specification, a rate against an item the bill did not ask for, or a quantity that does not tie back to the drawing revision the main contractor issued.

None of that is about how sharp your rates are. It is about whether the return is complete and internally consistent on the day it lands.

Quantities are measured to the drawings and the specification you send. Where the information is incomplete we list the assumption rather than absorb it.

Price is not the only thing being scored

Public frameworks and most main contractor selection panels weight quality alongside price - methodology, programme, resource and risk all carry marks. The exact split is set out in the tender documents for every job, and it is worth reading before you decide where to spend your effort.

What that means for the return

The parts of a bid that are easiest to skip - the method statement, the programme, the schedule of qualifications - are often the parts carrying marks. A bill that is priced but unqualified leaves those marks on the table, and leaves you carrying scope you never agreed.

Working from the model where there is one

Where the job comes with a federated model, we take quantities from it and check them against the drawings. Where it does not - which is still most subcontract packages - we measure from the 2D set. Either way the bill you get back is in your format and the assumptions are listed.

What We Bring to Your Tenders

Depending on what the job needs and what information the main contractor has issued, a submission can include:

5D cost models - measured quantities linked to the rates you price at

4D construction sequences - the method statement shown against the programme

Clash detection reports - coordination problems raised as tender queries, not absorbed

BIM execution plans - deliverables structured to ISO 19650

BREEAM and ESG cost lines - where the specification calls for them

Why this matters

Most subcontract packages still go out as a 2D set with a written specification. If you can show a main contractor how you intend to sequence and coordinate the work, and price it against quantities that tie back to their drawing revision, you are answering a question the other returns often leave open.

Methodology carries marks

The weighting given to technical methodology is set out in the tender documents for each job, and it is rarely nothing. A drawn sequence says more about how you will run the work than a paragraph does - how you phase it, how you get materials in, and where you need the preceding trade finished.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How is this different from traditional bid writing services?

A: Most bid writers focus on documents. We provide a complete system: strategic bid selection, BIM-integrated cost accuracy, methodology development, contract protection, and variation recovery. We’re not just helping you submit - we’re helping you win and profit.

Q: What win rate improvement can I realistically expect?

A: We do not publish a win rate. The outcome of a tender turns on your price, your programme and your relationship with the client, not on who typed the bill. What we can tell you is what leaves here: a bill priced against the specification, with the qualifications and the tender queries written down rather than buried.

Q: Do you work with smaller contractors and SMEs?

A: Absolutely. Many small/medium contractors lack in-house bid teams but need professional submissions to compete. We scale our services to match needs and budget. We’re experienced at making smaller firms appear as professional as major contractors.

Q: How quickly can you turn around a tender?

A: Ideally 3-4 weeks for comprehensive tender. For urgent opportunities, we handle 10-14 day deadlines. For extremely tight deadlines (7 days or less), we provide targeted support on specific elements. See our full bid process →

Q: What markets do you cover?

A: The UK, Australia and Poland. We measure to NRM2 or SMM7 in the UK, AS 4000 and AS 3700 in Australia, and KNR/KNNR for a Polish kosztorys. Tell us which your bill is written to.

Q: Can you help with framework applications?

A: Yes - frameworks require different approaches, demonstrating consistent capability and capacity rather than project-specific solutions. We’ve successfully supported applications to major frameworks. See framework services →
