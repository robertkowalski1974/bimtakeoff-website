Automated quantity takeoff uses BIM technology to extract material quantities directly from IFC model files produced by software such as Autodesk Revit, ArchiCAD, and Tekla Structures, reducing the need for manual measurement from 2D drawings. Quantities are extracted from the model with measurement software and then checked against the drawings by hand.

Quantities are measured to the drawings and the specification you send. Where the information is incomplete we list the assumption rather than absorb it.

How We Check Every Quantity

Software Extraction, Then Manual Checking

Construction projects cannot afford measurement errors. Every quantity goes through more than one check before it reaches you.

Step 1: Software Extraction

Automated BIM Analysis

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What if my project doesn’t have a BIM model?

A: We can work with 2D drawings (DWG, PDF) but timelines will be significantly longer as we’ll need to extract quantities manually. For best results and fastest turnaround, we recommend using BIM models or consider our BIM modelling services to convert your 2D drawings first.

Q: How accurate is your measurement process?

A: Quantities are extracted from the model with measurement software and then checked against the drawings by hand. Where the model is incomplete or ambiguous, we log the assumption rather than guess. We provide complete audit trails for all measurements.

Q: What happens if the design changes during the project?

A: BIM-based take-offs make this straightforward. We can update quantities based on the revised model, typically within 4-8 hours rather than weeks for manual re-measurement. We offer ongoing update services at discounted rates for active projects.

Q: Do you comply with international measurement standards?

A: Yes, we comply with RICS New Rules of Measurement (NRM), ISO 19650 BIM standards, and can adapt to local standards including Polish KNR, Australian AIQS, or any other regional requirements. We’re experienced with international projects and international measurement standards.

Q: Can you handle large, complex projects?

A: Yes. We’ve completed take-offs for projects ranging from small renovations to large developments. Our measurement software performs well on larger projects, where manual take-offs would be prohibitively time-consuming. Complex projects may require 3-4 days instead of 2-3.

Q: What level of detail (LOD) do you need in the BIM model?

A: We can work with LOD 300 and above for most projects. LOD 350-400 is ideal for detailed trade-specific take-offs. If your model is at LOD 200, we can still extract useful quantities but may need to supplement with 2D drawings for detailed measurements.

Q: Do you provide take-offs for specific trades or disciplines?

A: Yes! We can extract quantities for any trade: structural (concrete, steel, masonry), architectural (finishes, doors, windows), MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire protection), civil works, and specialist systems. We tailor the BOQ format to match your trade requirements.

Q: How do you handle model inconsistencies or errors?

A: This is why we check in stages. The measurement software identifies clashes and duplicates automatically. Manual review catches model errors and cross-checks with the 2D drawings. A final cross-reference check confirms logical consistency. We document all assumptions and flag any areas of concern for your review.

Q: What’s your pricing structure?

A: Priced per package, quoted before we start. No hourly billing and no fee for scope we did not agree. Each project is assessed individually based on project size, complexity, level of detail required, and specific deliverables. Contact us with your project details for a quote within 24 hours.
